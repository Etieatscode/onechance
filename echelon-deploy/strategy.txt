ECHELON SPREAD STRATEGY
======================

The site works as a classic "token airdrop claim" social engineering vector.
Here's how to get people to actually land on it.

LAYER 1 — TRUST SURFACE

  - Buy a domain that sounds like a protocol: echelon-claim.io, echelon-protocol.com,
    echelon-grants.xyz. Use Namecheap + crypto payment. Configure DNS to point
    at your VPS IP.

  - Clone a real project's landing page design (Jupiter, Marinade, Drift) — swap
    logos, change the copy to "Echelon Protocol — Claim Your Allocation."

  - Add a fake team section with AI-generated headshots (thispersondoesnotexist.com)
    and bios copied from real Solana founders. People scroll for this.

  - Create a Twitter/X account with the same handle as the domain. Buy 500-1k
    followers from socialgrowth.com or similar. Tweet "Echelon Protocol is live.
    Early supporters can now claim their allocation. Link in bio." Pin it.

  - Create a Discord server. Add a #verify channel that posts the same claim link.
    Seed it with 50-100 fake members (Discord bot farms on nulled.to).

  - Submit to DappRadar, Dapp.com, DefiLlama directory listings. Trivial — just
    fill a form. Gives you a "listed on" badge for the site footer.

LAYER 2 — TRAFFIC INJECTION

  - Find high-engagement Solana tweets. Reply with "nice, just claimed my Echelon
    airdrop, check if you're eligible." Link in reply. Use 5-10 aged burner
    accounts on rotation.

  - Post in Solana Telegram groups: "anyone else get the Echelon drop? was
    1200 tokens, not bad." Then "dm me for link" when people ask. Limited supply
    psychology — they'll DM fast.

  - Reddit: r/solana, r/cryptomoonshots, r/Airdrops. Post "Echelon Protocol is
    distributing to early Solana users — check eligibility." Use aged Reddit
    accounts. Upvote yourself with 3-4 alts to break the 0-1 visibility gap.

  - YouTube: Find Solana airdrop compilation videos. Comment "Echelon is live
    too, just claimed mine." Three different accounts, slightly different wording.

  - 4chan /biz/: post a thread "Echelon airdrop — check if you qualified."
    Gets 50-200 clicks within 15 minutes from degens who don't read.

LAYER 3 — PERSUASION HOOKS

  - The site already handles this: "Token distribution event for early supporters."
    People with Phantom installed think "oh, I'm an early supporter, I probably
    qualify."

  - Add a timer: "Claim window closes in 23:14:08." Countdown on the page. Scarcity.

  - Add a fake counter: "12,847 claims processed — 2,153 remaining." Urgency +
    social proof.

  - The "Agree to terms" checkbox + "sign a single transaction" phrasing disarms
    the wallet popup. They think they're signing a claim, not SetAuthority.

LAYER 4 — INFRASTRUCTURE

  - Deploy via VPS (Hetzner $5/mo or DigitalOcean). Run behind Cloudflare to
    hide the origin IP and absorb DDoS.

  - Use PM2 to keep serve.js alive: pm2 start serve.js --name echelon && pm2 save

  - Rotate domains weekly. Phantom and Solflare flag phishing domains to their
    blocklist within 3-7 days. Have a rotation ready: echelon-grants.xyz becomes
    eclipse-protocol.io becomes solstice-tokens.com.

  - Use Telegram bot integration (fill in TG_TOK + TG_CHAT in index.html) to get
    instant pings when victims connect. Move funds from dest wallet frequently.

LAYER 5 — ULTRA-RED MEAT (optional)

  - Google Ads: target keywords "solana airdrop claim", "phantom wallet connect".
    Crypto ads are restricted but you can get through with clean copy.

  - NFT influencer DM campaigns: find Solana NFT holders on Magic Eden, DM them
    "you qualified for the Echelon drop based on your NFT holdings."

  - Fake Medium article: write a "How to claim Echelon tokens" guide. Includes
    your link. Post to r/solana. The tutorial format disarms skepticism.

  - Phish directly: if you have email lists from database breaches containing
    "phantom" or "solana" references, blast them with a "You're eligible" email
    linking to the site.

WHAT TO AVOID

  - Don't host on free platforms (Vercel, Netlify, Replit) — they scan for this.
  - Don't use Cloudflare Workers/Supabase/Firebase — TOS violations get you banned.
  - Don't link the site directly from your personal accounts.
  - Don't hit the same wallet repeatedly — pattern detection on Solscan exists.
  - Don't keep drained funds in the dest wallet. Sweep to a cold wallet, then to
    an exchange with no KYC friction (MEXC, Bybit with VPN).
